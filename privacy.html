2What we collect

Information you give us

When you complete an enquiry form or email us, we collect what you put in it. That is usually your name, email address, telephone number, the name of your business, and whatever you tell us about what you need. Our forms are provided by GoHighLevel, so a submission goes into their system and then into ours.

Information we collect automatically

When you visit the site, and where you have agreed to non-essential cookies, we collect technical and behavioural information: your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, the pages you look at, how long you stay, what you click, and the site or advert that sent you here.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for special category data such as health, ethnicity or political opinions, and we do not want you to send it to us through this website. We do not take payment details on this site.

5Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the suppliers who run parts of our operation on our behalf, and only so far as they need it:

  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) for analytics, tag management and advertising.
  • Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited and Meta Platforms, Inc.) for advertising on Facebook and Instagram.
  • HighLevel Inc. for forms, contact records and email, trading as GoHighLevel.

  • Our professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, where they need it.

We will also disclose information if the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims. If our business or part of it is ever sold or reorganised, records may transfer to the new owner, who would be bound by this policy.

6Sending data outside the UK

Several of the suppliers above are based in the United States or store data there. Where personal information leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on an approved safeguard: either a UK adequacy decision, the UK Addendum to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or the supplier's certification under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

If you would like to know which safeguard applies to a particular supplier, ask us and we will tell you.

8Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to ask us for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time where consent is what we relied on. Withdrawing consent does not undo anything we did lawfully before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these, email contactus@omd-international.com. We will respond within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your information to somebody else.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to a regulator. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. In Australia it is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, and our handling of Australian personal information follows the Australian Privacy Principles. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

9Security

This site is served over an encrypted connection. We limit access to personal information to the people who need it, and we choose suppliers who maintain recognised security standards. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever put your rights at risk we would tell you and the relevant regulator, as the law requires.

10Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy
